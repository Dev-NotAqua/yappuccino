# Code of conduct

Yappuccino is made by people in their free time, for people who want a lighter
Discord. Keep it a place you'd want to come back to.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most rough edges are a missing word, not malice.
- Critique code, designs and ideas, never the person who made them.
- Help newcomers find their way: link the doc, explain the why.
- Respect a "no" from a maintainer on scope, and the time of people who review.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or sexual content aimed at anyone.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address, an account or a
  Discord token, without their consent.
- Asking for or sharing features that break Discord's Terms for others, such as
  spam tools, self-bots or account takeovers.

## Where it applies

Issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews on this repository, and the
project's Discord server.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/Dev-NotAqua/yappuccino/security/advisories/new)
(choose it even when it isn't a security issue), or to a moderator on the
project's Discord server. Reports stay confidential. Maintainers may warn,
remove content, or ban someone from the project's spaces, temporarily or for
good, depending on how serious and repeated the behaviour is.

This code borrows its shape from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/); the wording is
our own.
